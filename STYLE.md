# Bora y'all! — style is settled

Locked 24 Sep 2026. `template.html` is the source; `index.html` is generated by
`build.py` and must never be hand-edited.

**The daily sweep adds DATA, not design.** A sweep run changes `events.csv`,
`concelhos.csv`, `markets.csv`, `freenif.csv` — and touches `template.html` only to
add a town's coordinates to `TOWNS`. Any other change to `template.html` needs
Robert to ask for it. If something below looks wrong, say so in the report; don't
"improve" it on the way past.

## The decisions, and why

**Type.** Fraunces for display (logo, event titles, day headings), Manrope for
everything else, IBM Plex Mono for dates and counts. Seven sizes only — 11, 12.5,
13.5, 15, 16, 19, 25. Nothing between 12.5 and 13.5; near-identical steps read as
sloppiness, not hierarchy.

**Colour.** Cream `#F7F4ED` and deep blue `#123B62` in light; navy `#0A1D2E` in dark.
Both themes measured at **zero WCAG AA failures** — keep it that way, and re-measure
rather than eyeball after any colour change.

- `--mustard` `#FCAF15` is for FILLS and BORDERS ONLY. It measures 1.69:1 as text on
  cream. The town name used it and was the least legible thing on the page.
- `--ochre` is the text colour for town names and other warm accents.
- Category hues live in `--c-*` and are per-theme. They were fine all along; an
  earlier audit mis-read them because of the bug below.

**The `.top` trap.** `.top` is the class on BOTH the page header and every event
row's category strip. The header's sticky rule MUST stay scoped to `header.top`.
Unscoped, it made all ninety row strips `position:sticky; z-index:20` with a frosted
background — they pinned to the top of the viewport and bled through the real header.
The `@supports not (backdrop-filter…)` fallback goes with it.

**Layout.** Capped at 1240px. Past roughly 900px the row stops using the width and
everything beyond becomes dead air — at 1440 unscoped there was a ~740px empty band
across every row. Description column capped at `54ch`.

**The event row.** Date chip, then a 22px gap, then the content block. They were 12px
apart and read as touching. Row padding 12px top / 14px bottom. Category strip to
title 5px.

**"Mais Info ↗"** is its own grid child in the description's column, left-aligned
with the text above it. It has been wrong twice: right-aligned to the narrow column's
edge (landed in the gutter, anchored to nothing) and pinned to the row's far right
(buried). Aligned under the description is the answer. **Keep the Portuguese** — it is
deliberate. The markets and NIF cards say "source ↗" because there the link IS the
source citation; that difference is intended.

**Touch targets.** Every control clears 44px at phone width. The save star is padded
to 44×44 and pulls the extra back with negative margin so row height is unchanged.
Desktop keeps its compact chrome on purpose.

**Icons** are a Google font of ligatures and are hidden until the font proves it
loaded. Every icon-only button needs a `body.no-icons` fallback glyph — the star, the
theme toggle and `#nearMe` have one. Any new icon-only button needs one too, or it
renders as an invisible box.

**Footer** is three plain paragraphs. There was a tip-off form; it was removed on
purpose. Don't bring it back.

## Checks before shipping a style change

Render at 1440 / 834 / 390 in both themes and confirm:

1. `[...document.querySelectorAll('.ev .top')].filter(e=>getComputedStyle(e).position==='sticky').length` is **0**
2. Zero contrast failures at AA, both themes
3. Zero controls under 44px at 390px, and no horizontal overflow
4. Filters filter, all three tabs render, favourites toggle, mobile sheet opens
