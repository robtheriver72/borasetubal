Free entry with your NIF

Portugal's museums and monuments give free admission to residents on set days — you're usually asked to show your Cartão de Cidadão or quote your NIF (tax number) at the door or when booking online. Always double-check the current date on the venue's own site before you go.

Recurring markets

Bora — Portuguese for “let’s go”; é agora — “now’s the time”. Events are gathered daily from municipal agendas, venue programmes and listings sites across mainland Portugal — from Setúbal and Lisbon out to the Algarve, Alentejo and the north. Prices and dates come from the sources listed on each entry — check before you go. Categories: Local concerts are gigs at neighbourhood venues; Major concerts are touring acts at the big arenas and halls.
